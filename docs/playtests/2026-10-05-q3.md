# Q3 playtest, 2026-10-05

MIKE-16, MIKE-05, MIKE-06 and MIKE-07 pass in the shipped `qfiles/qa-c` built from `f5049e9a`, with both CPU and OpenGL rendering. [Recorded observations](2026-10-05-q3.json) include the actual Source state and audio output.

Both runs used the ordinary startup menus to choose Quake III Arena, its introductory `q3dm0` arena and Start. Real keyboard and relative mouse events went through SDL. The observer only read state. The player walked from the authored spawn to the shotgun, without teleporting, granting items or injecting gameplay events. Both sessions ended through the console with exit code zero.

| Item | Observed result |
| --- | --- |
| MIKE-16 | The menu published the connected Q3 player despite the unrelated invalid Q2 mod still present in discovery. |
| MIKE-05 | Mouse input changed pitch and yaw. Both Source angles and the rendered camera retained them after at least 650 milliseconds without further mouse input. |
| MIKE-06 | Pickup started the installed `sound/misc/w_pkup.wav`, with nonzero decoded PCM and mixer gain. The actual SDL device accepted non-silent PCM. No water sound was used for this pickup. |
| MIKE-07 | The shotgun inventory changed from zero to one and ammunition from zero to ten. The authored item became hidden, emitted `EV_ITEM_PICKUP` with its actual item index, and both Source and CGAME selected weapon 3. |

MIKE-16 was caused by `qa_catalog_clone` serializing and restoring a catalog, which rescanned every installation. An unrelated Q2 mod had a `maps` symlink outside its retained root, so that rescan failed while starting Q3. The clone now copies cached native metadata and retains its VFS mounts. It keeps filesystem containment and the unrelated product's unavailable state intact. Native ownership probes also passed after releasing the source catalog and at every clone allocation failure point.

Gameplay follows Quake III Arena's `code/game/g_items.c` pickup lifecycle and `code/cgame/cg_event.c` pickup sound and weapon selection. Engine paths are `src/gameplay/q3/items.c`, `src/presentation/q3_native/events.c`, and `src/app/application/native_q3_wire_state.c`.

These are local X11 checks of the reported introductory-arena behaviors. Audio used SDL's dummy output device, so the proof covers content decoding, the mixer and accepted device output, not physical speakers. The debugger and private display make these runs unsuitable for performance measurements.
