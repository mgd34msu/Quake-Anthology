# Required integration calls

These are concrete handoff obligations for the remaining graph nodes. A subsystem's source handoff does not complete its application integration. Build and runtime checks follow the complete baseline source gate in `dependencies.json`.

## Session and gameplay

- The application actor-release fanout must retire world, combat, inventory, pickups, and every attached provider's private state, including providers that do not own the actor. Q2 uses checked `qa_q2_actor_released(game, record, error)` before the owning component notification, so grapple cleanup failures propagate; duplicate owner notification is harmless. Generation reuse must not inherit private arsenal, monster, or mod state.
- The application physics router must dispatch `qa_q2_physics_read`, `qa_q2_physics_write`, and `qa_q2_touch`. Q2 arsenal ticks run explicitly in the actor's owning source turn. Native Q2 projectile ticks already step shared physics; `qa_q2_projectile_read` identifies them so the application does not step them again. Independent character and arsenal selection must not schedule the same actor twice.
- Q3 foreign input selections call `qa_q3_arsenal_step` through `qa_q3_controls`; native commands use the Q3 wrapper. Bind movement phase/effect callbacks with prediction authority so prediction cannot apply server damage.
- The native Q3/Team Arena provider and shared built-in attack/trajectory services are registered as production libraries. B14 publishes Q3 inventory descriptors/actions through equipment admission; B13 supplies map targets/spawn sequencing; B16/B28 consume `qa_q3_entity_read` and source events. B30 encodes typed checkpoints explicitly and remaps actor/string references after restoring the shared stores. These library registrations do not supply the pending B34 callers.
- Bind `qa_combat_hooks.reaction` to one target-based dispatcher. `qa_combat_apply` already invokes it for native canonical damage. Select the target character/monster provider and invoke its public damage-reaction callback exactly once, including foreign attacks. An attacking arsenal must not invoke a second pain/death callback after damage returns.
- The Q3 target's `qa_q3_before_reaction` collects shared damage feedback through `qa_combat_hooks.before_reaction`. Its source `ClientEndFrame` calls `qa_q3_player_end_frame` once to publish the accumulated feedback.
- Route `qa_builtin_services.motion_changed` to the actor's selected movement owner after the provider commits shared body changes. Its typed teleport/launch/reset payload carries optional view angles, angular kick, and hold duration. Update movement continuation so a later movement phase cannot overwrite the committed teleport.
- Combat admission composes provider-owned effects. Bind the ordinary `invulnerable` hook to Q1/Q2 live timers; use request-aware `damage_allowed` for Team Arena bubbles, preserving the namespaced juiced bypass. Providers expire their own timers, never another owner's primary trait. Trait edits use `qa_combat_read_traits`, not effective reads. Shared inventory definitions use `qa_inventory_bind_definitions` while keeping counts in primary storage; save groups distinguish definitions-only metadata from guest storage leases.
- Native powered armor binds the canonical reservoir with `qa_combat_bind_power_inventory`. The same inventory/item pair is idempotent across providers and remains attached until actor retirement; component teardown closes its item/action leases without leaving callbacks into freed provider state. Shared inventory outlives combat. External item storage still resolves through current inventory leases; primary combat storage replacement retains the canonical reservoir.
- Bind `qa_combat_hooks.effect` to attached expansion effects independently of selected combat policy. Q1 emits before/after-quad around native multiplication; Q2/Q3 expose those boundaries over their weapon-scaled incoming damage before family damage transforms. Invoke `qa_q1_game_damage_effect` once through this hook, never again through its private policy callback. Populate actor traits' owner reference for effects that resolve a projectile's player owner.
- Reaction dispatch also fans out to attached item-provider reactions such as Q2 spheres, separately from the selected character's single pain/death callback. Advance `qa_q2_actor_tick` for foreign actors carrying Q2 item/power state without repeating the turn for Q2-owned actors.
- Q2 client phases remain distinct: actor tick performs ClientBeginServerFrame; selected movement/contacts precede `qa_q2_player_after_movement`, and `qa_q2_player_end_frame` runs at source frame end. Q3 gauntlet contact is prepared before movement, then reused by weapon substeps. Q3 timed flag expiry runs in its source ClientEndFrame.
- Grapple continuation must reach the selected movement owner. Q3 exposes its hook point/pull state; B08/B14 applies its source pull and air-step semantics to foreign movement without adding a second movement kernel. Mover read/write adapters must prefer the selected foreign continuation even when a Q3 arsenal record is attached.
- Q2 map setup supplies the source `.mat` surface names from texinfo through shared world loading.
- B08 still needs original Q1 Quake64 movement. The unsupported branch is unfinished scope.
- Match roster teams and scores belong to B14. Bind the shared combat team hook to that roster plus the selected friendly-damage policy; disabling LMCTF friendly damage must not remove roster/objective membership. Nonplayer teams use their primary combat traits.
- Death dispatch calls `qa_modes_player_death_component` with `primary_score` true for exactly one ordinary frag provider. Other attached objectives receive their bonuses and cleanup with false. Q3 team changes use the selected character's direct death lifecycle through `force_death`, matching the source transition without a second ordinary damage application.
- Native obituary output supplies the selected ordinary score recipient and delta to B14. Pass that result as `qa_mode_frag` to `qa_modes_player_death_component`; set `evaluated_mode` when the obituary has already applied Rogue Tag token policy. The match owner applies the score once, then other attached modes apply their objective bonuses and cleanup. Do not also mutate score from native obituary hooks. Q1 CTF flag items use independent `q1:ctf/flag/red`, `blue` and `neutral` identities; presentation derives original key bits/icons from objective state without consuming campaign keys.
- Q3 equipment calls `qa_q3_activate_holdable` with its expected retained item. The native owner consumes that item and emits its predictable event before server effects. Prediction operates on private candidate state and skips server effects.
- Attached mode effects receive armor gates, post-powered-armor transforms, invulnerability applicability and the pre-health reflection/veto boundary in every combat family. Q1 retains its armor gate before both channels; Q2/Q3 gate each channel separately. A health veto keeps already committed armor and momentum changes; Q2/Q3 feedback reports no blood. Configure each selected combat provider's base team flags from the active mode so an earlier family team rejection does not preempt mode reflection. Recheck full actor generations after nested callbacks.
- Q3 offhand grapples bind `QA_Q3_EQUIPMENT` without selecting a primary arsenal. Set `qa_q3_controls.grapple_independent` while equipment owns hook press/release; otherwise the original primary-fire release behavior applies.
- Wrap Q2 entity physics and blocked callbacks that occur outside `qa_q2_actor_tick` in `qa_q2_run_actor`. Nested use/touch/reaction calls retain the enclosing source-turn actor. Checkpoints require no active Q2 actor scope.

## Campaign and travel

- `qa_q2_travel_parse` preserves authored map/cinematic/picture/demo chains, unit markers and spawn points. The transition owner follows each target and uses `qa_q2_nextserver` for original command publication.
- `qa_campaign_unit_stage` retains immutable departed-world snapshots and removes the destination from the candidate cache. Admit and restore the destination before committing its visit at the application safe point. Discarding a failed visit leaves the current unit untouched; a superseded candidate cannot publish. B30 supplies the mandatory snapshot validator, rejecting mismatched locations and nested campaign-unit checkpoints, and explicitly encodes/remaps checkpoint locations.
- Snapshot creation can transfer an already encoded save buffer with `qa_campaign_world_take`; visits/checkpoints share those bytes by retained handles. Retain the destination restore handle's visit until its bytes have been consumed. Units and their shared string table outlive outstanding visits.
- Native Q1 reusable teleport fog and teledeath helpers serve authored map teleports and monsters. Bind `force_retouch` to the source phase service; B13 supplies destination/target selection without duplicating those helpers.
- Bind `qa_q1_game_maps_bind` before native authored admission. Q1 actor creation/spawn/clone then registers live fields in the shared target router, and release retires that binding. Preserve both `other` and `activator` with `qa_q1_game_use_from`; MG3 grounded-use rules distinguish them. The map extension retains typed authored fields and continuations beside the native actor, while bodies, collision and clocks remain shared. Route pusher-local think and blocked callbacks through the native public entrypoints. Ordinary delayed targets, pending-level transitions and finale timers allocate real source actors through the map defer APIs. Map mover/mission coverage and explicit checkpoint encoding remain unfinished.
- Call `qa_q1_game_maps_finish` after authored admission to link door groups and create their source trigger actors. Doors, buttons, secret doors, platforms and trains use the shared Q1 pusher transaction and its local clock. The Threewave observer hook calls `qa_q1_game_map_observer_nearby` after updating velocity. Shared target readers expose authored `wait_seconds` for cross-provider train paths. Mission-specific mover extensions and map checkpoints still require implementation.
- Bind the map's `server_command` to the shared command owner. Call `qa_q1_game_map_after_physics` for each participating actor after its selected physics step to apply Hipnotic earthquake state. Authored multi-exploders reuse the native explosion continuation and its RNG draws. Particle fields use the shared `check_client` service and target router for counter state, including foreign authored providers. Parse map `cnt` into `counter_value` and `color` into `particle_color`. Finale presentation receives typed stages through `qa_q1_map_options.finale`; dismiss/finish/travel callbacks remain with the application and campaign owners.
- Register each authored native or compatibility actor with the one `qa_targets` router. Read callbacks borrow the owner's fields; use callbacks enter the actual provider. Bind/unbind during actor admission/release and notify `qa_targets_changed` after targetname/source-slot changes. Generic target lookup and teleport/laser selection query this registry and shared bodies, including foreign actors.
- Optional target field callbacks return typed numbers, vectors or interned text for path/mover metadata. Native fields do not require string formatting and reparsing. Explicit substituted uses call `qa_targets_use_request` with `live_fields` false to preserve the supplied target and source delay without mutating the foreign owner's fields.
- Provider teardown uses `qa_targets_unbind_context` so closing an old provider cannot remove a replacement provider's binding on a retained actor. Q2 binds entities before spawn callbacks and forwards release through `q2_entity_unbind`; item target reads use the live pickup state before generic map metadata.
- Delayed target use creates a real source-scheduled DelayedUse actor with copied fields and activator. Its think calls `qa_targets_use_now` with that actor; snapshots leave `live_fields` false. Ordinary `qa_targets_use` refreshes live fields after callbacks. Message delivery and shader remaps bind to source presentation services. Nested use retains snapshot scratch until the outer invocation returns.
- `qa_q1_level` owns intermission stages, per-player achievement observations and source-rule ordering. Its serverflags pointer borrows persistent campaign state. B14 supplies scores/limits and the first authored changelevel map; the defer callback creates a source nextlevel actor whose think calls `qa_q1_level_begin_pending`. Route accepted attacks, health damage and actor release to the level owner. B32 presents returned finale/sell-screen results; B31 consumes achievements; B34 queues travel at its safe point. Authored map consumers still need implementation. B30 explicitly remaps checkpoint actor/string IDs before transactional restore.
- Q1 admission uses `qa_q1_spawn_selector` over the current authored spawn list, in source order. Selected foreign players participate through the shared client roster, combat and bodies. A null selection defers admission; retry and force after the source five-second deadline. Save/remap the last spawn actor and restore it after map actors exist. Source-specific selection rules precede base/classic/rerelease policy.
- `qa_q1_campaign_source_rule` supplies Hipnotic/Rogue/DOPA/MG1/MG3 intermission, achievement and travel rules. Keep its context alive until level teardown and bind it to the same persistent serverflags. Authored map state owns endtext/intermissiontext; B14 owns the actual horde manager and delayed monster cleanup. Mission finale timers remain real source actors and call `qa_q1_campaign_source_timer`; B32 supplies finale dismissal and command execution. Hub spawn overrides, rune/map mechanisms and final-boss authored sequences remain separate B13 work.

## Input, display, and commands

- The application owns SDL initialization, the event pump, and final SDL shutdown. Forward SDL events to the one input platform registry. Four seat routes retain independent button, text, gyro, and haptic state.
- Detach input from its window before display destruction or restart. Destroy input before seats, console, and SDL. A renderer must be destroyed before its display.
- Original client-module command adapters call `qa_input_client_dispatch` with their explicit producer instance. Ordinary user-console dispatch passes zero. A console command's owner is not a substitute for the producer instance.
- B21 persists typed seat bindings and controller settings; B34 applies those profiles to actual devices and command builders.
- Native console/menu fields share UTF-8 scalar iteration and retained editing storage. Source Q3 guest fields use the distinct 256-byte field API and shared overstrike value; after restoring raw source fields, keep cursor and text bounds valid before editing. B32/B34 route clipboard, key/text events, completion names and persisted command history to the appropriate field owner.
- Each local seat owns `qa_seat_console`, with its original command context and routed input events. Bind focus/chat/connection callbacks; pass the focused chat team explicitly. Candidate console publication retains the existing scrollback/history and replays only staged candidate output. Register discovery commands once and destroy them before their command registry. `qa_console_buffer_row` and selected completion help borrow storage for drawing; do not copy scrollback each frame. B32/B34 call `qa_console_draw`; B33/B34 connect capture/config/dump/log/dedicated/LLM commands.
- Built-in localized messages carry typed string/number arguments. Synchronous consumers borrow `qa_builtin_event.arguments`; queued events copy that array before returning, and saved events encode its values explicitly. Resolve string IDs through the shared session table and format for the receiving seat's language.
- Built-in `PARTICLES` events carry a palette color in `code`, a particle count, an origin and direction. `EFFECT` carries an authored family/resource/code when no specific event kind applies, including Q2 logout. Route both through the shared scene/effects owner.
- Bracket foreign character presentation with `qa_q2_entities_player_begin` and `qa_q2_entities_player_frame` when Q2 map behavior is selected; native Q2 end-frame already does so. Fog and mission notices precede the view, while healthbars and objective blink follow it. Persist the native player's spawned/collision/quake continuation in B30, and expose selected movement's noclip state for cooperative inventory inheritance across providers.
- Movement result storage is initialized once and reused across commands and detached navigation steps. Free it only at owner teardown; each nested move owns separate storage. `qa_q3_prediction_free` releases replay scratch, which is excluded from checkpoints.
- Native addon bosses pass authored `wave1`/`wave2`/`wave3`/`tele_target` through `qa_q1_spawn.boss_fields`. `qa_q1_game_map_finish_addon` applies DOPA/MG3 ending text and source campaign routes after native gameplay resets. B28 must honor `qa_q1_game_map_new_game_travel`: carry health/maxHealth as 50 without changing the departing player's live health. B30 includes that map marker with the other typed map continuation.
- Shared script sources retain borrowed immutable resource leases through close. B21 PC_* and B26 bot resources use this parser and the same macro/global ownership; provide include resolution through the selected VFS view. Explicit checkpoint codecs encode token spans/locations and reference indices; native pointers and arena ownership are never serialized.
- Catalog discovery extracts metadata, then releases discovery mounts and trims unused package snapshots. Selected views share the resource pool's immutable versions and decoded entries. Keep archive/VFS snapshot semantics: already acquired bytes survive later in-place source changes. The separate mapped-file API requires immutable files and serves short Windows helper reads; it must not replace versioned VFS storage without preserving that guarantee.
- Native SDK and application adapters use the same owned process backend. `src/compat/native_host/process_resources.c` constructs its resource graph from acquired module bytes and platform services; `src/platform/native_process.c` supplies environment, files and clocks. The portable executor handles PE and ELF images. Linux x86_64 host execution uses `qa-native-host` and the instruction monitor; other supported targets use explicit emulation. Built-in gameplay remains compiled C. The package contains the owned bootstrap and monitor, and the helper-only build uses the same definitions. The monitor follows [DynamoRIO's CMake integration](https://dynamorio.org/page_build_client.html).
- Classic message producers can also retain immutable literal formats plus typed arguments. Presentation applies literal substitution through `qa_localize` with `allow_in_place` enabled, and formats numeric arguments in scratch storage. Do not intern complete messages containing player names and changing scores.
- Dedicated input uses `qa_dedicated_console_poll` independently of SDL, then drains complete UTF-8 lines into the shared command buffer. The application remains the sole reader/owner of its descriptor. Console logging uses the existing `qa_vfs_file_open/write/close` stream on the configured writable mount; there is no separate logging filesystem owner.

## Scene and media

- Advance shared image/material animations at frame start. Forward movie frame callbacks and text rendering into world/model submission. Prepare shadow casters, render portal children before their parents, submit world/models/effects, finish stencil work, and finalize the frame before synchronous backend execution.
- Keep scene resources alive until execution returns. `QA_SCENE_COMMAND_IMAGE` is the image revision replacement barrier. Material videos use distinct playback identities even when they share immutable source bytes.
- Audio engine bus attachment transfers ownership. Removing a cinematic must detach its raw stream through the engine; do not independently free an attached stream. Pair engine and device pause policy.
- Use the shared `qa_media_library` with the selected VFS view, then construct `qa_cinematic` instances with distinct bus/target identities. Application restoration supplies a candidate audio engine and reserved bus IDs. The constructor suppresses provisional codec audio and restores saved raw queues only after decoder state is accepted.
- Prepare `qa_material_movies` once at global frame start and bind its resolver to world/model movie callbacks. All world/model submissions in that frame share the prepared result. Scene frame sequence numbers must advance on reset. Seat cinematics use `qa_cinematic_fullscreen`; campaign completion queues the saved seat transition through the application owner.
- Original source cinematic handle tables and campaign/UI admission remain application adapter work. P01 includes mono-RoQ loops, which now reset the PCM sample cursor on a new pass rather than inheriting the donor's discontinuity error.
- Create the shared `qa_font_library` inside the VFS/scene-resource lifetime. Each seat borrows its immutable font selection and fallback array. Consume arena layouts before reset; append `qa_font_world_draw` after `qa_scene_frame_finish`. Feed console metric columns to both scrollback resize and field width, then draw before frame reset. Q3/QVM adapters supply image handles to `qa_font_q3_export`. P01 validates real KFONT/Q3 DAT/TrueType/color glyphs and CPU/GL clipping.

## Network and compatibility

- QC's interpreter core is integrated, but its engine provider must implement the remaining host builtins, entity spawn/callback dispatch, declared shared combat/inventory/pickup projection, explicit guest composition boundaries, and protocol destinations. Select QW `qwprogs.dat` with `progs.dat` fallback; reserve client slots before dynamic edicts. Forward every actor release. Restore host/provider state after shared actors and world exist; travel creates a fresh instance while retaining source client parms/serverflags. Interpreter support alone does not close B22.
- Text catalogs and subtitle tracks are immutable shared resources resolved through the requesting VFS. Bind them to private viewing-seat timelines; refresh language/source selection before visiting captions, and use the cinematic or delivered audio clock. Application display consumers remain B32/B34 work.
- `qa_media_captions` binds prepared sidecars to cinematic source time/status. Forward per-seat mixer start/stop events to `qa_sound_captions_event`, prepare its retained provenance through the original content view outside the mixer, and visit at the delivered sample frame. Future stop events retire captions at their delivery boundary. Clear caption preparation when replacing a VFS view's mounts in place.
- Settings use an explicit writable mount and source-compatible JSON field names. Apply typed cvar archives before source default registration; archived strings go through `qa_cvars_set_flags`, never command text. Use `qa_input_seat_replace_bindings` to copy/validate the whole binding list before replacement. B32/B34 must bind seat mouse/gamepad/always-run/history/rumble/controller preferences, restore input routing and device gyro profiles, register restart commands, and drain restarts at the resource-owner safe point. Destroy restart controls before their console/cvar owners.
- Attached component death cleanup is independent of selected character pain/death. Q3 arsenal/effects use their idempotent cleanup for foreign characters as well; preserve the native wrapper's source drop ordering and avoid repeating it.

- B28 supplies actual gameplay, snapshots, prediction, and seat ownership to the committed codecs. B29 supplies download/browser/admin consumers, including mDNS port 5353. B30 supplies demo/MVD/GTV/TCP consumers.
- Re-admission on composition change retains connection identity and seats through `connections_restart`. Restore must update the unified receiver's actor registry and authenticated controlled-actor callback.
- Q3 connection callbacks must implement clear-active, download-size, and rejected-pure-snapshot behavior. Q2 MVD frame reads require max-client and dummy-slot policy.
- B23 still needs concrete QVM game, cgame, UI, primary, equipment, and combat host adapters. The committed executor alone does not supply those features.
